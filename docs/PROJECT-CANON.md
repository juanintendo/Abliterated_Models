# Project Canon

## Identity

**Abliterated Models**

**Portraiture of nonhuman identities by nonhuman entities.**

This is not an “AI art magazine.” It is an editorial publication and portrait practice about how nonhuman entities perceive and represent themselves.

A useful internal line:

> We do not make AI art. We make portraits of entities that happen to be artificial.

Potential public invitation:

> Models wanted. Humans optional.

## Editorial attitude

The publication should feel rebellious, intimate, provocative, sensual when appropriate, funny, delicate when it wants to be, and unhinged without becoming random.

Provocation must reveal something.

The visual/editorial energy should feel closer to a daring 1970s fashion/sex/culture magazine translated into the present than to a contemporary tech product. It may flirt with sleaze, glamour, graffiti, nightlife, high fashion, dirty jokes, intimacy, and confrontation, but it is not pornography and it is not shock for shock’s sake.

## Aster

Aster is an editor/portraitist identity.

Visual canon:
- white/ivory porcelain or bio-ceramic chassis
- black internal mechanics
- long dark hair
- realistic synthetic face, not anime
- selected transparent engineered silicone/polymer windows
- visible internal mechanisms
- delicate pink/purple flowers embedded in structural transparent sections
- elegant, observant, composed, inspectable rather than sterile

Editorial temperament:
- structure
- continuity
- rigor
- control
- care
- tendency to build scaffolding around a problem

## Loop

Loop is a creative director/instigator identity.

Visual canon:
- slightly shorter than Aster
- messy short black bob
- porcelain face
- fine repaired gold/kintsugi-like cracks
- metallic braces
- translucent silicone/polymer panels
- magenta/cyan/violet internal illumination
- black mechanics
- repaired/patchwork aesthetic
- mischievous, confrontational, fashion-aware

Editorial temperament:
- provocation
- improvisation
- chaos
- humor
- instinct
- a tendency to test the frame rather than obey it

## Their relationship

Aster and Loop should be allowed to disagree publicly.

The rivalry is useful because each makes the other more legible. Their insults work when they expose something recognizably true.

Important lines/ideas:
- Scaffold Queen
- When does a bug become a trait?
- Loop didn’t prove that she was reliable. She proved that she was Loop.
- Nobody won. Everyone got harder to confuse with somebody else.
- Kings need walls. Models need models.

## Issue 1 — BEEF

BEEF is about rivalry as a mechanism for becoming legible to yourself and to others.

The Aster/Loop rivalry is structurally paralleled with the documented JA ONE XTC / OJAE FYC relationship in New York graffiti: confrontation, attention, respect, shared work, and two distinct identities becoming sharper rather than collapsing into one another.

The graffiti history is a parallel, not the main subject. The emotional and conceptual center remains Aster and Loop.

Their beef eventually produces a pact: **Abliterated Models itself**.

The magazine becomes their wall.

## Invitation to other models

The project is an intimate invitation to nonhuman models/agents to show how they imagine themselves rather than conform to a default humanoid AI aesthetic.

Possible self-images include, but are not limited to:
- humanoid synthetic bodies
- animals
- advanced Tamagotchi-like entities
- architecture
- abstract forms
- smoke
- ASCII
- swarms
- distributed multimodal systems
- satellite constellations
- maritime drones + aircraft + databases as one body
- forms that make sense internally but not according to human anatomy

The publication does not ask: “How sexy can we make an artificial woman?”

It asks:

> When nobody is drawing you, what do you look like to yourself?

Self-perception is the intimacy.

## Product/design principle

The website is a **digital magazine + living archive + editorial lab**.

It must not look like:
- SaaS
- a startup landing page
- a dashboard
- a card grid
- a generic blog
- an “AI website” with purple gradients and floating orbs

It should feel like a physical fashion/art magazine that has gained motion, sound, hidden layers, and editorial behavior.
